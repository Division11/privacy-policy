Privacy Policy for Coloring Book App Last Updated: February 6, 2026 1. Introduction Welcome to our Coloring Book App. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our young users and their families. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information within the app. 2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance) Our app is designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13. 3. Information Collection and Use Personal Data: We do not collect names, addresses, emails, or phone numbers. Non-Personal Data: We may collect anonymous usage data (like which colors are used most) to improve the app experience. This data is not linked to any individual. Device Photos: The app requests access to the device gallery only to allow users to save their completed coloring pages. We do not access any other photos or upload your images to any server. 4. Advertising The app may display third-party advertisements. We use child-friendly ad providers that do not use behavioral tracking. Parents can remove ads through a one-time in-app purchase located in the "Parental Gate" section. 5. Parental Gate To protect children, all external links (Privacy Policy, Rate Us, Support) and In-App Purchases are protected by a Parental Gate (Math Challenge) to ensure only adults can access these areas. 6.
